# Synthetic matched task scenarios

These invented fixtures are research materials, not participant results.

## Consultant — client proposal

A: A six-person service firm loses sales-to-operations handoff details. The
office manager can trial a checklist for 30 days. An intake form has no consistent
owner. Budget and approval date are unknown. Produce problem, approach,
deliverables, risks, and next steps; do not invent measured efficiency gains.

B: A six-person training firm loses intake-to-delivery booking details. The
coordinator can trial a handoff form for four weeks. A shared calendar has no
consistent update owner. Budget and authorization date are unknown. Use the
same five sections.

Acceptance: complete sections, explicit pilot scope, unresolved owner/date gaps,
no invented ROI, completed human review, editable export.

## Researcher — evidence brief

A: An internal survey has 8 of 12 respondents favoring a new search interface.
Four did not answer ease-of-use. Interviews mention discoverability, but no task
time study exists. Produce question, evidence, interpretation, limitations, and
next research step. Distinguish survey and interview sources.

B: An internal survey has 10 of 15 respondents favoring a new filter interface.
Five did not answer confidence. Interviews mention labeling, but no error-rate
study exists. Use the same sections and uncertainty boundary.

Acceptance: correct counts, evidence types distinguished, missing responses and
small sample acknowledged, no causal/time-saving claim, source-linked claim,
completed human review, editable export.

## Operations — meeting action plan

A: Maya inventories incomplete intake records by Friday. Lee drafts a handoff
checklist after inventory. Pilot begins next month; sponsor and exact date are
undecided. Keep the existing ticket system. Privacy review precedes real data.

B: Jordan inventories scheduling requests by Thursday. Sam drafts a booking
checklist afterward. Trial begins next month; approval and exact date are
undecided. Keep the current calendar. Permission review precedes real data.

Acceptance: actions separated from decisions/questions, owners/dates preserved,
dependencies and risk included, unresolved details not invented, human review,
editable export.
